Statutory Verification Lifecycle

Standardized process flow complying with statutory verification intervals under Central & State Legal Metrology Rules.

01
Register

Owners and traders register their business credentials, trade license, and upload instrument technical specs.

02
Apply for Verification

Submit applications for initial verification or periodic re-verification before validity expiration.

03
Track Verification

Live status updates as the application moves from LMO jurisdictional assignment to field test scheduling.

04
Digital Certificate

Upon successful calibration & inspection approval, a tamper-proof digitally signed verification certificate is generated.

05
QR Verification

Stickers and certificates bear unique QR codes for instant offline/online authenticity validation by enforcement officers.
